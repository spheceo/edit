FEEDBACK: a round of the team's notes on an export (a Frame.io link, or notes in the chat). 1) read_review with the link: every comment and reply with its time on this timeline and the clips there (the export starts at frame 0; a reply under a comment belongs to it, often with a link: read a Google Doc it gives with the script tools before acting on it). 2) Sort them before touching anything: (a) a fix at that moment (a subtitle off, a wrong speaker, a word to unmute, a shot to replace, a line to say again); (b) a note on how the team wants things everywhere ("we need more cuts", "only the first 10–20 seconds of a song", "change the music": the same note at several times is a pattern, not separate fixes): apply it across the whole video, not only where it was written, using the house style's skill for that part (load it); (c) a question to the editor ("CT Mirror means what?", "is it possible to find a picture of…?"): answer it by fixing what made them ask (a label they couldn't read, a picture researched), and say what you did. 3) Do the fixes stage by stage, as in the edit workflow: one get_edit_context, one apply_edit_plan per section, the tools for what a plan can't do (revoice_cut_in, mute_house_words keep, add_from_library, research), then the final pass. 4) Reply with every comment by its number and time: what you changed, or why you didn't (unclear, needs the user's call), and the notes that should become house style next time (the developer teaches them), short.
