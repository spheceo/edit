description: The last review of a whole edit and the fixes it finds.

THE FINAL PASS: one review_edit with frames across the whole edit (one per section and each place an earlier stage flagged; at most 24). Its audit reports gaps, slivers, bare cut-in cards, floating clips, exhausted sources, misaligned sound and uncovered speech; the frames are for what only eyes catch: redactions covered, captions or subtitles on the wrong person, a subtitle or caption over someone's face (the audit's text_on_face; place_subtitles or place_captions fixes it), transitions that repeat, a face of a minor showing. Its audit also flags B-roll that's mostly pixelated or blurred (censored_broll; while censor_check_pending lists files the check isn't done: review again before you finish), quote callouts away from the words that point to them (callout_off_speech), footage that doesn't play its script range whole (off_script: realign_script fixes it), captions or subtitles that aren't what's said (text_not_said: fix the words from read_transcript), words under a blur (text_under_blur), B-roll shots held too long (long_broll_shot) and music that outstays its song, repeats or stops under the narration (music_bed_long, music_repeated, music_stops_mid_narration: music-and-sound); footage cut inside a word (cut_mid_word: move that cut to where the word ends, word_ends, or starts; Corrupt Cop's clip ended before "sir" was finished: "let the clip play a bit longer here so he finishes the 'sir'"), a desktop profile's line typed away from the words that say it (profile_line_off_speech: its said_at is the frame), another channel's call to action on screen (source_cta: a rect blur adjustment over its blur_box, project pixels from the centre, for its start–end, on every stretch it lists: "Subscribe should be blurred here. This could appear throughout the video"), a B-roll shot that runs across a cut in its footage (broll_crosses_cut: start it after the cut or end it before; a court stream switching to a document mid-shot reads as "images randomly pop up") and, in a courtroom video, callouts, tags and labels too small to read (small_text: bring them to at_least), and a subtitled line under an opaque black solid (line_under_black: the black is silent; play the line in picture before it). While picture_check_pending lists files, those checks aren't done: review again before you finish. Fix what you find in one apply_edit_plan (tidy_tracks included), then run mute_house_words over the whole timeline last, once. Don't re-review sections you already checked unless you changed them.
