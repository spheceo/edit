THE FINAL PASS: one review_edit with frames across the whole edit (one per section and each place an earlier stage flagged; at most 24). Its audit names the mechanical problems: FIX THEM ALL, because the team finds every one left; leave one only when it can't be fixed, and say why. Where the fix isn't in the issue: off_script → realign_script; cut_mid_word → extend_clip (no arguments: every one); letterboxed → op fill_frame; watermarked_picture / watermarked_footage / source_cta → crop or punch in past it, else a rect blur; burned_subtitles → the scale and y it names; subtitle_unheard → delete it; unsubtitled_speech → subtitle or caption it (the teasers too); vignette_snaps → op ease_vignettes; dead_air → remove_time; text_late, subtitle_late → start it at start_by (a run of late subtitles moved off their footage: add_subtitles again over that span); glimpse → a picture seen under half a second between two others reads as a glitch ("really, really small clips like this, where it almost seems like a glitch… are removed"): end the shot where the one over it starts, or hold the one either side over it; silent_popup, silent_transition → a click, whoosh or riser on it. While picture_check_pending or censor_check_pending lists files, review again before finishing. Every script note starting "edit:" is done where it asks. The frames are for what only eyes catch: a face covered by a subtitle, caption or window (place_subtitles, place_captions), captions on the wrong person, a minor's face showing, a picture too dark or blurred to see who's in it, transitions that repeat. Fix what you find in one apply_edit_plan (tidy_tracks included), then run mute_house_words over the whole timeline last, once. Don't re-review sections you didn't change.
