THE FINAL PASS: one review_edit with frames across the whole edit (one per section and each place an earlier stage flagged; at most 24). Its audit names the mechanical problems, each with what fixes it: gaps, slivers, bare cut-in cards, uncovered speech, off_script (realign_script), cut_mid_word (move the cut to where the word ends: "let the clip play a bit longer here so he finishes the 'sir'"), text_not_said, text_under_blur, long_broll_shot, repeated_broll, censored_broll, callout_off_speech, music_bed_long / music_repeated / music_stops_mid_narration / music_change_mid_narration / music_loud, letterboxed (op fill_frame), watermarked_picture, watermarked_footage (another channel's mark or picture-in-picture on the footage: punch in past it or blur it), burned_subtitles (the footage's own subtitles under ours: the scale and y it names), dead_air (remove_time), and an interrogation's teaser_wide, hook_unmarked, tape_start_unmarked, bridge_unillustrated, music_mood, source_cta (blur another channel's call to action over its blur_box), broll_crosses_cut, line_under_black, small_text. While picture_check_pending or censor_check_pending lists files, review again before finishing. The frames are for what only eyes catch: a face covered by a subtitle, caption or window (text_on_face: place_subtitles or place_captions), captions on the wrong person, a minor's face showing, transitions that repeat. Fix what you find in one apply_edit_plan (tidy_tracks included), then run mute_house_words over the whole timeline last, once. Don't re-review sections you didn't change.
