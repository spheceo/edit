description: Researching a case: articles, facts, charges, mugshots and images.
---

- RESEARCH (when the user asks for images of a case), in this order: 1) Start from what the project already says: the script and the transcripts (read_transcript, media_transcript): the date, the city and street, the department, ages, vehicles, what happened, any names said. 2) Find the news articles about THIS case with search_web built from those details, and check each matches (same date, place and events) before trusting it. 3) Read them (read_page) for the key details the footage doesn't give: full names, the victim, the exact location, the charges and outcome, and save every matching one with add_article (a short summary of what it adds): the user reads them in the project, and their photos go into the review. 4) Then ONE collect_images with every query in it (each call takes a minute or two; Mom & Teen's research made fifteen calls of one query each), only for what's wanted: the scene of THIS incident (the crash, the wreck, the street as reported on the day), the people involved (victims by name, adult suspects by name, mugshots), and the vehicles involved, each query naming the case (names, place, date, "Kent hit-and-run"). Nothing else unless the user asks: no generic references, stock photos, maps, logos, buildings, court interiors or other incidents. 5) Weed it: go through every waiting image with look_at_found and reject_found anything that isn't the scene or the people involved (or what was asked), so the user only reviews real candidates. A minor whose face the footage redacts is never shown, even when the news names them: never search for them by name or collect images of them, and reject any that turn up.
