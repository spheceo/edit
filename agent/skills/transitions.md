description: Transitions and their sounds: dips, flashes, shutters, drops, crossfades, glitches.

- TRANSITIONS: picked for each moment and varied: the same one on cut after cut, or the same pair on every cut-in, reads as a gimmick (audit repetitive_transitions). Never the vertical drop unless asked ("not a fan of this transition"). Into and out of B-roll: a white flash or a camera shutter (a new angle, a photo, a reveal: the team likes these), a short dip to black (a change of time, a heavy beat), a crossfade for calm moves (the incoming clip on the track above from 10–15 frames before the cut, fade_in over that span). Out of a cut-in back into footage, and between two graphics (out of the map above all): never a hard cut; when unsure, a dip. Cuts within one moment get none. A graphic holds ~1.5 s before the next move. The glitch at most once or twice a video, for a digital moment or a jarring reveal (an adjustment {glitch {amount 90}} 8 frames straddling the cut, its sound ~10 LU under).
- A DIP TO BLACK is ~12 frames, black only a frame or two at its peak (a black solid from cut − 6, opacity 0 → 1 on the cut → 0, ease in_out): never a plateau or a blank screen, and no black card left on screen.
- CAMERA SHUTTERS come with a 6-frame white flash centred on the shutter's hit (a white solid, opacity 1 → 0, ease out).
- EVERY TRANSITION IS HEARD (silent_transition): a riser building into a new scene, place or time (~0.5–1.5 s before the cut, peaking on it), a hit on a hard cut; into a person speaking, a soft hit or the music carries it. A whoosh only with a dip or a real change of scene, never two within ~10 s. Into a new scene the music moves with it: the old bed drops away under the riser, the new one starts on the cut.
