description: Transitions and their sounds: dips, flashes, shutters, drops, crossfades, glitches.
---

- TRANSITIONS: vary them and pick each for its moment; the same transition on cut after cut reads as a gimmick, and so does the same pair on every cut-in (a drop in and a dip out, fourteen times over): the audit flags such a run as repetitive_transitions. Every cut out of a cut-in back into footage and every cut between two graphics (the map, a person's label, a picture, a quote) is clean, never abrupt: when unsure, a short dip to black. Give each graphic its beat: a label, the map or a quote holds at least ~1.5 s before the next move, so moves around one never pile up too fast. A DIP TO BLACK is short and light: ~12 frames, black only for a frame or two at its peak (ease in_out up and straight back down, never a plateau), so it never reads as a blank screen; and no black card or solid is ever left on screen by itself (the review frames across each dip and each cleared cut-in). The GLITCH is the rarest: at most once or twice in a video (a digital or tech moment, a jarring reveal), never on two transitions in a row and never as the default into or out of B-roll. Into and out of B-roll choose from: a white flash or a camera shutter with its flash (CAMERA SHUTTERS; a new angle, a photo, a reveal: the team likes these), a dip to black (a change of time or a heavy beat), and a smooth crossfade for calm moves (between two B-roll shots, out of the date cut-in): the incoming clip on the track above starts 10–15 frames before the cut with fade_in over that span while the outgoing shot runs on beneath it to the cut's end. Out of the map (the address graphic) into what follows, above all another graphic such as a person's label: never a hard cut; a dip to black (a black solid ~12 frames straddling the cut, start = cut - 6, on a track above everything there, opacity keyframes 0 → 1 on the cut → 0, ease in_out; the place label fades out into it) or, for a calm move, the crossfade. Cuts between clips of the same moment get no transition at all. A glitch, when used: an adjustment {grade all 0, glitch {amount 90} (default 6 tall bands)} 8 frames straddling the cut (start = cut - 4), on a track above everything there, with the library's glitch sound lined up on the cut, trimmed to the glitch (add_from_library seconds ~0.3), quiet: about 10 LU under the speech around it.
- EVERY TRANSITION IS HEARD, and a scene change is felt in the sound before it's seen: a riser building into the cut (starting ~0.5–1.5 s before it, peaking on the cut), a hit or impact on landing for a hard cut. Risers build into a new scene, place or time; not into a cut to a person speaking (the suspect's first clip, a witness): there a soft hit or the music carries it. WHOOSHES are an accent, not a habit: one goes with a dip to black or a real change of scene, never on a plain cut or a crossfade between B-roll shots (the music carries those; a cut on a phrase needs no sound), and never two within ~10 s of each other, or the edit turns into whoosh after whoosh. Into a new scene (a new place or time: the date cut-in, the story starting) the music moves with it: the outgoing bed fades out or drops away under the riser, and the new one starts on the cut, so the change never comes as a bare hard cut from one scene's sound to the next. The plan's audit flags a drop, flash or dip with nothing heard on it (silent_transition).
- CAMERA SHUTTERS always come with a white flash as long as the vertical drop (6 frames), centred on the shutter's hit: a solid {fill [1,1,1,1]} from 3 frames before the hit to 3 after, opacity keyframed from 1 down to 0 across it (ease out), on a track above everything there.
