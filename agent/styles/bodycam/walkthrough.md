A WORKED EDIT: Welfare Check (21:17, 10 voice-overs; Amanda and Alan charged). What the finished timeline holds, stage by stage, and the calls that get there. The rules are the skills'; this is what following them looks like.

1. RESEARCH: the articles saved (one search, the pages read, add_article each) gave the date and the road (Springhill Church Rd, on the date cut-in's map), the charges word for word, and who's who; the boys are minors, never shown. Later stages read them back in one call: case_articles.
2. THE INTRO: the script's teasers in its order, the first narration in at 9.3 s. One get_edit_context (section intro, transcript), one apply_edit_plan for the teasers' cuts, captions and the open, one look at the plan's review frames.
3. THE CUT-INS: nine, each 4–8 B-roll shots under ONE B-roll look and one zoom (cover_cut_in), a new shot every 2–4 s: the patrol cars at the house, the front door, the dark hallway, the bedroom, the trash on the floor, the tent in the boys' room, the locked room. One find_in_footage with a phrase per cut-in found them all; a sheet of only the stretches it named settled the doubtful ones. The date cut-in: the date title over the darkened picture. Cut-in 3: the Windows screen with a Notepad of four lines typed as the narration says them, a click as each window opens. Every cut is heard: dips (10) and flashes (9), each with a riser or a camera shutter. The two boys' faces: a blur following each head (track_region); the bodycam's timestamp: a blur on it in its three stretches. One apply_edit_plan with review_frames on each cut-in's first shot.
4. THE OUTRO: the outcome narration over the last shots, the charges as the articles give them.
5. SUBTITLES: watch_footage (cached by then), add_subtitles over everything after the intro with neglect on (a neglect case), one label per person by role (Deputy:, Deputy Ensley:, Amanda:, Alan:, Patricia:, DCF:, Paramedic:, Brandy:, the boys as Victim: and Victim 2:), each in their colour; only the watcher's doubtful lines looked at.
6. MUSIC AND SOUND: list_library once, then one batch: a new bed for each cut-in (13 in all, never one song twice running), under_speech, a fresh opening each time.
7. THE FINAL PASS: one review_edit with frames across the video; every audit issue fixed in one apply_edit_plan; mute_house_words over the whole timeline last.

What JJ still caught on it (20 notes), so it's done first time: words to mute in the footage ("feces* - (mute feces)", "mute \"children\"": mute_house_words with the case's words), and the music: "we want to loop the first part of the song rather than having this right here - we dont like this specific part of the song" (a song's opening under each cut-in, never its middle).
