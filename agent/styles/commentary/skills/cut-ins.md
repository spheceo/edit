description: Cover commentary chapter narration with relevant footage and smooth editorial graphics.
---

Every narration span needs a picture chosen for its meaning. cover_cut_in can place clean footage shots, but explicitly set look false and into none for commentary, then use commentary_look only on the narration span. Its generic picture/desktop mode belongs to other styles: use commentary_card for a still, screenshot or inset video instead. Follow the narration's actual word times. Aim for a new meaningful picture or composition around every 3–6 seconds, allowing longer evidence reading and deliberate slow pushes. Don't cut to an unrelated shot just to satisfy a timer. A long source excerpt (>20s) needs motivated 120% punch-ins/out or gentle moves, not a decorative transition across each sentence. Remove source silences over 1.5s without clipping words.

commentary_card {media,start,end,source_in?,background?,label?,x?,y?,width?,height?,backdrop?}: clean rounded, glowing white framed picture with an ease-out slide and subtle drift, sharp over a dark blurred copy or brand wallpaper. width/height are maximum shares of the canvas (.82/.77); x/y project pixels from centre. backdrop:false adds a second card over an existing composition. Label real source metadata only. commentary_text {text,start,end,motion:soft|slide|pop|typewriter,blur?,size?,x?,y?,box?,align?}: selective editorial phrase, a chapter's few key lines, never every sentence; it sits on the B-roll, which stays seen (blur:true dims and blurs the picture behind it: rare, at most a quarter of the narration); soft is a character opacity sweep with stable full-phrase geometry, slide/pop move the complete phrase. For a person+list composition use commentary_profile {media,name?,background?,start,end,lines:[{text,at}]}: a portrait left, short fact bullets right, at in absolute frames as each fact is said. Its complete final list keeps the left edge stable as it reveals. Do not reveal the entire list before it is said. A screenshot is real evidence, cropped to the relevant region; highlight only its actual words, never redraw its content as if original.

commentary_chapter {text,number,start,end} is an episode-neutral chapter slate, ideally over a chapter turn already in the narration rather than an extra silence. Chapter names follow the Writer headings. Brand wallpaper or a real folder asset can replace its background; never retain another episode's baked title. commentary_look {start,end} is slight grain/vignette and belongs only on narration visuals. Avoid stacking it with another grade. Hard cuts and smooth compositional changes do most of the work; occasional top-to-bottom image slides, no perpetual flash machine. End graphics and blur with their own shot.
