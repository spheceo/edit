description: Commentary transitions use strong eased card motion, occasional image slides and restrained light leaks.

A visual change follows a named thing, evidence reveal or story turn. Hard cuts between source scenes; no compulsory flash at each narration paragraph. Card slides/pop scales use strong ease-out (commentary_* operations), a short whoosh/click with the transient at arrival. A typewriter gets quiet keyboard sounds. A serious turn may get a bass hit, never constant booms. The channel's light leak is commentary_leak {at, media: brand_light_leak}: the real stock film burn, rotated, Screen, 350%, 10 frames before and 12 after the cut, exactly the reference's recipe (one Evil Design episode used it 58 times). Use it rarely, on a chapter's start or the story's biggest turn, always with a soft whoosh at the cut ("Light leak with the sound effect"; "we are not going to have light leaks for most of the video"). Nearly every change is a clean cut; where two shots need softening, transition kind dissolve fades the incoming shot in over the outgoing one ("either a clean cut without the morphing between, or we put a clean cross-dissolve"). A slowed shot ends before a cut in its footage, never across one: smoothing across a cut morphs the two scenes into each other. The generic light_leak transition is another style's. A shutter sound needs a flash on screen with it (transition kind flash) or a photo landing on that frame: "if we're having camera shutter effects without any sort of flash on screen, that is weird." Keep chapter transitions coherent and sparse.
