A visual change follows a named thing, evidence reveal or story turn. Hard cuts between source scenes; no compulsory flash at each narration paragraph. Card slides/pop scales use strong ease-out (commentary_* operations), a short whoosh/click with the transient at arrival. A typewriter gets quiet keyboard sounds. A serious turn may get a bass hit, never constant booms. The channel's light leak is commentary_leak {at, media: brand_light_leak}: the real stock film burn, rotated, Screen, 350%, 10 frames before and 12 after the cut, exactly the reference's recipe (one Evil Design episode used it 58 times). Use it on story turns, chapter starts and the intro's beats, always with a soft whoosh or shutter at the cut ("Light leak with the sound effect, we're not using that"). The generic light_leak transition is another style's. A shutter sound needs a flash on screen with it (transition kind flash) or a photo landing on that frame: "if we're having camera shutter effects without any sort of flash on screen, that is weird." Keep chapter transitions coherent and sparse.
