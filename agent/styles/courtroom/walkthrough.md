A WORKED EDIT: Trooper FAKES Testimony (20:08, five voice-overs in the courtroom voice). The hearing carries it; the narration only explains. What the finished timeline holds, stage by stage, and the calls that get there.

1. RESEARCH: the hearing's date, the court, Sgt Williams's name and what he testified to, from the saved articles; the dashcam the case turns on is in the footage.
2. THE COLD OPEN: 36 s of the hearing's strongest lines in the script's order, opening on a dip from black, the first narration after it. One get_edit_context (section intro, transcript), one apply_edit_plan, one look.
3. THE HOOK AND THE SETUP: the setup cut-in ("On <date>…") is the long one, each shot held under its push: Williams sworn in and on the stand, the dashcam rolling (the black pickup at the stop sign, the Malibu, the paused frame), the courtroom, and the Windows screen with a Notepad of the four facts the narration lists. One find_in_footage for every bridge at once.
4. THE HEARING AND ITS BRIDGES: long stretches of the hearing (1–3 min) between three short bridges of one to three held shots each (the dashcam's own data, lights 1 and siren 0; the frozen frame at the stop sign; the judge), cuts heard as light leaks with a dark whoosh. Other channels' logos on the court feed: blur_marks once, which laid 134 tracked mark blurs, each starting and ending with its shot (never a still rectangle laid by hand). One apply_edit_plan per stretch, with review_frames on each bridge's first shot.
5. THE OUTCOME: the outro's sentencing card.
6. SUBTITLES: watch_footage, add_subtitles from 0 (the teasers too), then the labels taken off and capitals on (the courtroom's subtitles carry no names), review_edit once for uncovered stretches.
7. MUSIC AND SOUND: list_library once, five beds, one per section, low under the hearing, never over a ruling; one batch.
8. THE FINAL PASS: review_edit with frames across the video, everything it lists fixed in one plan, mute_house_words last.

What Marius caught on the edit before it, so it's done first time: "Really loud sound effect for no reason" (a whoosh under a teaser), the logo blur staying on through a transition, clicks under the narration. And JJ, on courtroom edits since (Nov 7): B-roll cut "almost every second" between punch-ins of the same camera "makes it all feel cheap": hold the shot and push in on it.
