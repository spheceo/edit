A HORROR VIDEO is a narrated internet-mystery documentary: disturbing posts, reels, deleted videos, lost media and the stories behind them, told by the team's narrator (the voice-over recording is given; never generate one when it is there). It is built with the commentary tools (commentary_*, highlight_evidence, import_broll, capture_evidence), but it is not a Janitor video: no bumper, no brand wallpaper or light leak, no corner labels for comic beats, no hype punch-ins. Each script's lines that end on a link ("Take a look at this:") are the story's own content: that post plays there.

**The look is Edit's, on everything.** "Always use grain and vignette on all footage, it's extremely important for the atmosphere we create": commentary_look over the whole video (Edit's analog look: grain, vignette, chromatic aberration, sharpened faces, saturated colour) once the pictures are placed, and again after adding more; the audit's horror_look_missing says where it's missing. "Screenshots… should never look modern… the whole style centers around analog horror and VHS-like effects": every screenshot in commentary_card is shown as its retro copy (softened, a faint grid, roughened edges, drop shadow), made by Edit. B-roll never gets the grid. A tall reel or phone video placed full frame stands over its own blurred, darkened copy by itself: place it as it is, never with black bars and never cropped to fill.

**Pace.** "Instead of hyper-retention-focused editing, we take a slower approach, so animations can take a bit longer than in other videos and give each moment room to breathe. This doesn't mean showing things for longer than necessary, though. Once a shot or animation has made its point, move on."

**Sound.** "Profanity ban list, it doesn't matter whether it's on voice over or clips, these need to stay out of the video": mute_house_words over the whole edit silences horror's list (robbed, kill, die, death, dead, drugs, murder, shoot, torture, gun, sex, molest, rape, slurs… and their other forms) in the narration and the clips alike. "Instead of using a classic 'beep' effect… we just want to cut the audio and leave empty silence where the profanity once was." On-screen words keep their first letter and stars.

**Sensitive pictures.** Gore, bodies and graphic injuries are censored. A real death is shown up to the moment before, never the moment itself.
