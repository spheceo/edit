description: Cover every horror narration span with the story's own content, retro screenshots and slow, deliberate graphics.

Every narration span gets the picture its words name, chosen with the research ladder: the post or video itself, then the account, its bio, its comments and the news about it. Change the picture when the words move on, not on a timer: "once a shot or animation has made its point, move on". When the line before a link says to watch ("Take a look at this:", "like this:"), that post plays full frame with its own sound for the seconds that show what was said, cut to where it makes the point; otherwise it plays silent under the narration. Never loop one post under a whole case: show its different moments, then its profile, then what people said.

**What Edit does by itself** (don't build these): a tall reel or phone video placed full frame stands over its own blurred, darkened copy; every screenshot in commentary_card is its retro copy with roughened edges and a faint grid; the analog look goes over everything with commentary_look. Place pictures plainly and let Edit dress them.

The guide's animations, each with its tool:
- **Screenshots and posts (Animation 12):** commentary_card on the screenshot (a profile, a bio, a comment, an article, a tweet), cropped to what the line is about, with footage still playing under it: the card darkens it, "B-roll never gets the grid". It slides up slowly with a blur that clears as it lands.
- **The highlighter (Animation 4):** "the one we use most": highlight_evidence on the card's photo over the exact words the narration quotes, red with roughened edges; "sometimes we zoom in while doing the highlighter effect" (the card's push, up to 1.15).
- **Pictures (Animation 5):** commentary_card with photo: true for a photo of a person or place: a white outline with a glow and a drop shadow, sliding up.
- **3D (Animation 6):** "When showing images or news articles… try turning them into a 3D layer and adding a slight rotation": now and then commentary_rotate on an article's screenshot.
- **Text slide-up (Animations 2 and 7):** "Whenever we switch to a new topic or introduce a new chapter", the case's title ("Case 2: Jack") as commentary_text motion slide with blur: true over its first footage; without the blur for a smaller beat; dates slide up the same way.
- **Emphasis synced to the voice-over (Animation 9):** "whenever we want to put more emphasis on certain words… or when a section needs a bit more visualization": commentary_text motion soft, the key phrase only, a few per case.
- **Typewriter (Animation 13):** "to emphasize a short phrase of 2–5 words or to quote someone": commentary_text motion typewriter, with a quiet keyboard sound.
- **A person and what they are (Animation 10):** "an image on the left or right with text on the opposite side… perfect for quoting someone or adding short bullet points that describe a person": commentary_profile.
- **An icon with its label (Animation 11):** "an icon with text below it… especially when representing people (STALKER)", a phone icon for "Leaked messages", a folder for "Court documents": commentary_icon.
- **A turn (Animation 8):** "the blackout transition… a slow fade through black": transition kind dip with frames 24–36, on a case or chapter change, not between every shot.

Animations "can take a bit longer than in other videos": a card holds while it's read (at least 3 s), a text slide-up arrives over about a second. Nothing is shown "for longer than necessary". After composing each case, check_shots over it (two minutes at a time) and replace every shot whose picture doesn't show what its words name.
