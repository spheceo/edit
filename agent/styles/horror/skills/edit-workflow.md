description: The complete horror edit, from the script and recording to the finished, reviewed video.
---

Do the complete edit in this turn, stage by stage. One context read, one plan and one look per stage; batch independent source imports and timeline operations. Finish every case or chapter, not just an elaborate intro. Keep the script's order and the recording's words; its notes and links are instructions to the editor, not spoken text.

1. Load research. Read the script once: every link and the line it follows is a beat. Import every post the script links in one or two import_broll batches (a whole post each; posts are short), and capture_evidence for profiles, comments and image posts. Then look at what came in and record_broll_observations, with one sentence each on why it fits its line.
2. Load intro and transitions. The intro opens on the story's most striking moments, then the narrator's hook, ending on the question the video answers.
3. Load cut-ins. Work case by case (or chapter by chapter) with get_edit_context narrowed to its seconds. Every narration span gets the picture its words name: the post itself first, then screenshots of the account, its bio, its comments, the news, in one atomic plan per case. Then check_shots over the case and replace what doesn't fit.
4. Load outro. Finish on the narrator's last line with a slow fade through black.
5. Load subtitles. mute_house_words over the whole edit (narration and clips: silence, never a beep); censor sensitive pictures.
6. Load music-and-sound. Level the clips' sound to the narrator; a low, dark bed under the narration only; a whoosh on each slide up.
7. Load final-pass. commentary_look over the whole video last (every frame, footage and clips), then review_edit, the audit and one motion sample; fix what's concretely wrong. Share for review when asked, and return the real link only once it's ready.
