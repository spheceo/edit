description: The complete horror edit, from the script and recording to the finished, reviewed video.
---

Do the complete edit in this turn, stage by stage. One context read, one plan and one look per stage; batch independent source imports and timeline operations. Finish every case or chapter, not just an elaborate intro. Keep the script's order and the recording's words; its notes and links are instructions to the editor, not spoken text.

**A script whose comments direct the edit** (a note on most lines: "Animation 4: … highlight …: <link>", "B-roll: … (0:08-0:20)", "Show the channel screenshot from drive here, zoom in slowly"): "comments are so important we need to implement every single one perfectly". Each note is the shot for the words it sits on: do exactly what it says, with the source it links, at those words, in its order; the skills' own choices fill only the lines with no note, and never replace one. A linked video's range: import_broll exactly that range and look at it frame by frame (look_at_media over the range, every frame of a short one) before placing it, so its crop, punch-in, blur or cut point lands where the note says ("crop the AP logo", "cut before ~0:23", "skip the title card"). A Drive link is the writer's own file (import_broll it); an article or archived page is capture_evidence; an X post is tweet_reference and commentary_tweet. The Animation numbers are the cut-ins skill's. A chapter title note ("Chapter title (on screen, not voiced)") is commentary_chapter there. The audit's note_link_unused lists each linked source not on screen yet: none is left at the end but those that can't be had, each said with why.

1. Load research. The narration first: cut-ins with no voice-over (the import couldn't make it) get generate_voiceover before anything else, unless the user gave the narrator's recording (place_voiceovers it); never edit over silent cards. Read the script once: every post it links is already on the timeline where it's linked, whole, with its sound, between the narration's cut-ins (the import did that); a post it couldn't get (a picture-only post, one behind a sign-in) is in its notes. Look at each post once, then source what the narration needs beyond them: the accounts, bios and comments it describes (capture_evidence), the news about it, the picture-only posts as screenshots. record_broll_observations with one sentence each on why it fits its line.
2. Load intro and transitions. The intro opens on the story's most striking moments, then the narrator's hook, ending on the question the video answers.
3. Load cut-ins. Work case by case (or chapter by chapter) with get_edit_context narrowed to its seconds. First cut each laid-out post to the seconds that show what its line said (a few seconds to ~15; a long one in two or three bites), tighten_voiceover footage:true after. Then every narration span gets the picture its words name: the post's other moments, then screenshots of the account, its bio, its comments, the news, in one atomic plan per case. Then check_shots over the case, two minutes at a time, and replace what doesn't fit.
4. Load outro. Finish on the narrator's last line with a slow fade through black.
5. Load subtitles. mute_house_words over the whole edit (narration and clips: silence, never a beep); censor sensitive pictures.
6. Load music-and-sound. Level the clips' sound to the narrator; a low, dark bed under the narration only; a whoosh on each slide up.
7. Load final-pass. one apply_edit_plan with the operation commentary_look {start: 0, end: the last frame} last (every frame, footage and clips), then review_edit, the audit and one motion sample; fix what's concretely wrong. Share for review when asked, and return the real link only once it's ready.
