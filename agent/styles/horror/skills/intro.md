The intro's opening slots ("1. (0:00–0:01) + (0:58–1:03)") are the cold open: the most disturbing seconds of the story's own posts, in the order given, each cut where it hits, with their own sound and no music. An empty slot means picking one from the strongest moments yourself. Then the narrator's hook: show each post and account it names, its real footage, at the words that name them. The video's title line ("Today, we'll take a look at…") gets its own centred text slide-up with the background blurred. Let the last line breathe, then a slow fade through black into the first case.
