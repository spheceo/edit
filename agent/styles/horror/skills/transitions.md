description: Horror transitions are clean cuts and slow fades through black.
---

Nearly every change is a clean cut. A case or chapter change, or the story's biggest turn, gets the blackout transition: "we fade the first image or video down to 0% opacity, then fade the second one up from 0% to 100%, creating a slow fade through black" (transition kind dip, frames 40–48: about three-quarters of a second down and as long up, as Sirius and Nbody fade). No light leaks, flashes, glitches or drops: the slowness is the style. A card or text slide-up carries the whoosh (whoosh-3) with its loudest moment on the arrival, "not a regular slide up": a fast start that settles slowly, as commentary_card and commentary_text already move.
