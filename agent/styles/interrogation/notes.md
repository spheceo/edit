INTERROGATION VIDEOS: a fixed camera on an interview room, the person questioned facing it, the detectives mostly with their backs to it. The video is that footage, mostly raw: stretches of one to three minutes as the Doc's ranges cut them, between short narration bridges (7–30 s) that say what the footage doesn't. The room carries the video; graphics are for the opening and for what the room can't show.
- SEVERAL CASES: a script can be a compilation (several cases, one Doc, or a Doc whose notes bring in each case's own script). Each case has its own footage, research, opening teasers and hook, date cut-in (its first narration after its teasers, or after "Moving on to the next case…") and outcome narration, which is that case's outro wherever it sits. Work stage by stage, every case in a stage before the next stage (get_edit_context with start/end per case). A narration whose note says "Reuse: <link>" is followed by a finished video of ours, imported whole: nothing over it, no flash out of it, set captioned (set_segments) before subtitling.
- "Note to editor: Blur this" on a narration word ("murder", "killed") means the word is censored, never a blur on the picture: starred wherever it's on screen ("m****r") and muted in the voice-over (final pass). Murder, blood and strip are starred everywhere even unmarked.
- THE OPENING (the teasers, their hooks and each case's backstory, up to the frame the tape rolls) is built the way Red Thread opens: graphics on screen most of the way (60–75%, never more than ~5 s without one), then none once the tape rolls (intro skill).
- AFTER THE OPENING the footage plays clean, as in bodycam: no punch-ins or effects; only the bridges and outros get treatment. Every line said in the footage gets bodycam's subtitles, in the speaker's colour (subtitles skill). Every narration is entered and left with THE BRIDGE FLASH; everything else is a hard cut (transitions skill).
- THE BRIDGES (the cut-ins) are built as the cut-ins skill says, footage first: cover_cut_in with look: false (natural colour), into: "none", speed 1 (the room never needs slowing) and ONE zoom across the whole bridge (push [1, 1.08]). The shots are mostly the room itself (the person questioned listening, rubbing their face, a detective sliding a photo across; another camera angle when there is one), never a moment before it has played (a confession, a breakdown, a photo being shown). A graphic only where the words name what the room can't show (the scene, a victim, the past, a record, a timeline): a film-look photo, a red quote, the Windows screen (at most one bridge in three), an evidence board for evidence, a map with File Viewers for a place.
- THE NAME CALLOUT, once per person questioned, when the narration first names them over their footage: full name and age ("Steven Mark Zelich, 54") small beside their head: white Geist 500 ~26, glow off, background {color [0,0,0,1], opacity 0.8, pad_x 10, pad_y 6}, typed on (typewriter, stagger 1), a thin white leader from the neck (leader {color [1,1,1,0.9], width 2, attach "side"}, track_region target "leader" on their face), held ~3 s, fade_out 6. A detective the narration names gets the same with their role ("Det. Nicona Cordero").
- THE CLOSING LINE ON SCREEN (two or three a video, the strongest hooks): a bridge's last sentence typed on as it's said, over the picture blurred and darkened (an adjustment {grade {exposure -1.2}, blur {radius ~40}} beneath it), white Geist 700 ~62, centred, max_width 0.8*width, glow, typewriter in step with the voice, held to the bridge's end.
- MUSIC: under the opening, the bridges and the outros only: a low dark bed that comes in with a bridge's flash and fades out (~1 s) across the flash back into the room. The raw interrogation has no music: the room's sound carries it.
