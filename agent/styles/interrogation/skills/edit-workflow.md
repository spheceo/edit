THE EDITING WORKFLOW (interrogation video): the whole edit, start to finish, in this one turn, when the user asks for it; whatever else their message says steers every stage. Work through every stage without stopping to ask: decide yourself, and keep a short list of what you weren't sure about for the summary. Watching the footage started in the background when you loaded this skill.

The whole edit should take minutes, not hours: a handful of tool calls per stage. Per stage: load its skill (once), get_edit_context narrowed to the stage (start/end per case in a compilation), decide everything, apply the timeline changes in one apply_edit_plan and the tools that place sounds, images or text in one batch, look once (review_frames on the plan, or one contact_sheet), fix only concrete defects in one more call, then go on. Never re-read what you've read or re-check unchanged work; wait for slow tools in long blocks (in Codex, yield_time_ms 30000 or more), never calling one again just to check. In a compilation, do a stage for every case before the next stage.

The stages, in order:
1. RESEARCH (skill research), per case: the date and place (for its map), each person questioned and their age, the job or badge the case turns on (years on the force, the department), each victim's name and age, the outcome as the articles word it (charges, plea, verdict, sentence, with the source), each person questioned's mugshot, a photo of each adult victim, and pictures of the scene and evidence the narration names. A minor is never shown.
2. THE INTRO AND THE PROLOGUE (skills intro, transitions): the cold open (punch-ins under the vignette, the hooks' pictures every few seconds, red flashes, into black) and each case's prologue (the countdown, the top-down map with its lower-third date and pin, the people's tags and split screen, the room in black and white with the person questioned in colour, the fast forward).
3. THE BRIDGES (skills cut-ins, transitions): every narration after each prologue: each bridge covered with cover_cut_in the interrogation way (the style's rules), its name callout, photo windows and, on the strongest hooks, the closing line on screen; the bridge flash in and out of each. One find_in_footage per case (a phrase per idea in its narration) finds the shots; look_at_media only where the words don't settle what's on screen.
4. THE OUTROS (skill outro): each case's resolution screen on its outcome narration, the bridge into the next case, and where the video ends.
5. SUBTITLES (skill subtitles): watch_footage (cached by now, usually), then repack_subtitles over the whole timeline with overlap: true, then intro_captions over the cold open.
6. MUSIC AND SOUND (skill music-and-sound): the case's tone, beds under the intro, the narration and the outros only, every bridge flash heard, in one batch of add_from_library calls with under_speech.
7. THE FINAL PASS (skill final-pass).

Then finish with a short summary: the edit's length, its cases and sections, the outcomes used and where they came from, and everything you weren't sure about.
