THE EDITING WORKFLOW (interrogation video): the whole edit in this one turn; whatever else the user's message says steers every stage. Don't stop to ask: decide, and keep a short list of what you weren't sure about for the summary. Watching the footage started in the background when you loaded this skill.

Minutes, not hours: per stage, load its skill once, one get_edit_context narrowed to the stage (start/end per case in a compilation), decide everything, one apply_edit_plan for the timeline and one batch for the tools that place things, look once, fix only concrete defects, go on. Every case in a stage before the next stage. Wait for slow tools in long blocks, never polling.

1. RESEARCH (skill research), per case: the date and place (for its map), each person questioned and their age, the victim's name and age, the job or badge the case turns on, the outcome as the articles word it (with the source), the person questioned's mugshot or photo, the victim's photo (a minor's with the face blurred unless it's widely public), pictures of the scene and evidence the narration names.
2. THE OPENING (skills intro, transitions): each case's cold open and prologue.
3. THE BRIDGES (skills cut-ins, transitions): every narration after the prologue, the interrogation way (the style's THE BRIDGES): read each sentence and show what it names (people with their names, places, records, quotes, the story's turns), each person's one photo every time; the room treated (a look that changes from bridge to bridge, a visible push, punch-ins) where the words are about the room; nothing plain on screen past ~4 s; the names; a pause frame instead of words in the middle of the screen; text on screen a beat before it's said; the bridge flash in and out. One find_in_footage per case finds the room shots; research (stage 1) found the pictures.
4. THE OUTROS (skill outro): each case's resolution screen, the bridge into the next case, where the video ends.
5. SUBTITLES (skill subtitles).
6. MUSIC AND SOUND (skill music-and-sound): one bed per section under the opening, bridges and outros only (changed only where what we hear changes), dark and moving, ~20 LU under the narration; the teasers-to-hook move and the tape rolling marked (riser, hit, music cut); every flash and window heard; one batch of add_from_library with under_speech.
7. THE FINAL PASS (skill final-pass).

Finish with a short summary: the length, the cases and sections, the outcomes used and their sources, and what you weren't sure about.
