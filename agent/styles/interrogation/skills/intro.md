In a compilation every case opens this way. Each teaser ends on a finished sentence.
- THE COLD OPEN (the teasers and their hooks):
  - Straight onto the first teaser, no opening dip. Each teaser is a STATIC PUNCH-IN on the person questioned (scale ~1.3, their head and shoulders a little off centre, no scale keyframes) under one heavy dark vignette over all the teasers (an adjustment {grade {vignette 0.7, contrast 0.1}}).
  - THE TEASERS' CAPTIONS: once the subtitles are on, op intro_captions {start 0, end: the prologue's first frame, emphasis: the subtitle clips of the lines that land (the lie, the reveal, the threat: one or two a teaser)}: the lines come up word by word as they're said, flush left, in bodycam's caption look and each speaker's colour, the emphasis lines in red ("it's not a red background, it's the red text").
  - The hooks are on the Windows screen (cut-ins skill), changing on what the narration names: the room's footage zooms out of a Video Player onto the desktop, the person questioned's photo or mugshot opens in a Photo Viewer as they're named, the victim's, the scene's and the documents' as they're named, the key facts typed in a Notepad as they're said ("3 months helping detectives", "never mentioned Jonathan"). Then back to the next teaser on a hard cut or a red_flash. At most one text_slam in the cold open, on the story's turn ("THEN THE CASE GOES COLD").
  - Cuts: hard cuts between teasers and screens, a red_flash (op transition {kind "red_flash", at}) on the hardest beats. The cold open ends on a red_flash into ~1 s of black on a low sub hit. THE BLACK IS SILENT: nothing said or subtitled under it (the audit's line_under_black).
- THE PROLOGUE (the case's backstory and date cut-in, up to its first interrogation range): op countdown {start, end: the frame the tape rolls} over all of it, and:
  - THE MAP top-down and flat ("just a flat angle"): map_view of the place with pitch 0, the building centred and glowing red (map_view does both); date_title {position "bottom", place "City, State", text: the date} as the lower third. No pins, photos or lines on the map.
  - THE PEOPLE AND THE STORY on the Windows screen: the victim and the person questioned in Photo Viewers as they're named, what happened typed in a Notepad, the room's footage in a Video Player; end it zoomed into the Video Player of the footage that comes next (exit_into).
  - THE ROOM introduced: the interrogation in black and white with only the person questioned in colour (an adjustment {grade {vignette 0, vignette_saturation -1, vignette_size ~0.45, vignette_aspect ~0.7}}, track_region target "vignette" on them), their name in big faint letters top left (white Geist 300 ~90, opacity 0.6, typewriter) as the narration names them.
