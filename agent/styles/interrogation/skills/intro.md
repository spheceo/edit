- THE OPENING (interrogation), built the way Red Thread Interrogations opens (the team: "leaning a lot more on the Red Thread interrogations stuff"): teaser lines from the interrogation (the Doc's first ranges) with short narration hooks between them, then THE PROLOGUE (the case's backstory) up to the frame the tape rolls. In a compilation every case opens this way. Each teaser ends on a finished sentence.
- THE COLD OPEN (the teasers and their hooks):
  - Straight onto the first teaser, no opening dip. Each teaser is a STATIC PUNCH-IN on the person questioned (scale ~1.3, their head and shoulders a little off centre, no scale keyframes) under one heavy dark vignette over all the teasers (an adjustment {grade {vignette 0.7, contrast 0.1}}).
  - THE TEASERS' CAPTIONS: once the subtitles are on, op intro_captions {start 0, end: the prologue's first frame, emphasis: the subtitle clips of the lines that land (the lie, the reveal, the threat: one or two a teaser)}: the lines come up word by word as they're said, flush left, in bodycam's caption look and each speaker's colour, the emphasis lines in red ("it's not a red background, it's the red text").
  - The hooks' pictures, changing every few seconds on what the narration names, each a different graphic: the person questioned's photo or mugshot under the film look as they're named (op film_look, "[ACTUAL PHOTO]"), the victim's the same way, the family's in black and white with a slow push; a red quote of the line that gives them away (op red_quote); a record or the facts the hook lists on the Windows screen once (a desktop shot: the room zooming out into it, Photo Viewers, short Notepad notes typed as they're said). Never the same picture twice. At most one text_slam in the cold open, on the story's turn ("THEN THE CASE GOES COLD").
  - Cuts: hard cuts between teasers and screens, a red_flash (op transition {kind "red_flash", at}) on the hardest beats. The cold open ends on a red_flash into ~1 s of black on a low sub hit. THE BLACK IS SILENT: nothing said or subtitled under it (the audit's line_under_black).
- THE PROLOGUE (the case's backstory and date cut-in, up to its first interrogation range): op countdown {start, end: the frame the tape rolls} over all of it, and:
  - THE MAP, as the team's map references: map_view of the place, top-down (pitch 0) or a gentle angle, tone "dark", the building centred and glowing (marker "building") or a glowing dot (marker "dot"); date_title {position "bottom", place "City, State", text: the date} as the lower third; op place_label typing the place's name in monospace; the people the narration names over it in File Viewers (op file_viewer, their names in caps on them), as they're named.
  - THE STORY behind the case on the Windows screen when it's a list of facts or several people at once; otherwise film-look photos.
  - THE ROOM introduced: the interrogation in black and white with only the person questioned in colour (an adjustment {grade {vignette 0, vignette_saturation -1, vignette_size ~0.45, vignette_aspect ~0.7}}, track_region target "vignette" on them), their name in big faint letters top left (white Geist 300 ~90, opacity 0.6, typewriter) as the narration names them.
  - fast_forward over the room played at 4–8× (its sound muted) when the narration skips hours; timeline_bar when it spans years.
  - The countdown reaches 00:01 on the prologue's last picture; then the tape rolls raw. No disclaimer unless the user asks for one (then op disclaimer over the last ~5 s before the tape).
