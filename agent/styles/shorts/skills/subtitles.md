add_subtitles over the whole Short: the house subtitles ("the same subtitles as we do right now for the body cam"), which in a 9:16 project come a word or three at a time, a little below the middle (above the player's title and buttons), each under its speaker's label in their colour. Label who's who as in every edit (Officer, Suspect, Victim, Driver; a name when the video says it). Never move them to the bottom.

Action lines: where something the story needs happens without words (running, a chase, a fall, a door kicked in), add one line saying it, in the words of the reference ("Running Away", "Chasing", "Falls Down"), as a subtitle of that person: the same style and label as their subtitles (copy one of theirs in apply_edit_plan and change its text), over the moment. Two or three a Short at most.

THE CALLOUT: once (twice when two people carry the story), as the person the story is about first appears clearly: shorts_callout with who they are in two or three words ("WANTED SUSPECT", "DRUNK DRIVER", "ENTITLED DAUGHTER"), at the frame they're clearest, point on the top of their head. Look at that frame once: the arrow ends on them and the words don't cover their face.

mute_house_words once at the end: the swears muted in the sound and starred on screen (s***, a**), as in every edit.
