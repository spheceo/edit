# The Disturbing Rise of N3on — v1 notes (first 6 minutes)

The user's notes on [v1](https://feedback.spheceo.com/r/4DDZ9gNBeBwIOfZnI-hfTA) (R3 with the recorded VO, project `p1792955140698-155`). Their overall verdict: "for most, the overwhelming majority, the B-roll has just not been done well at all", and "lean in more on our reference motion graphics… instead of recycling the same ones."

## Patterns

### 1. The same few sources, and pictures that don't fit the line
"We're using all of the same clips." "We're not using appropriate B-roll." The narration says he faked his death, and none of the shots are the videos where he did ("A lot of people who have watched his videos would know, 'Oh, these are the videos in which he faked that'"). "We say he meets with the most famous people on the planet, and we show someone who I personally have never even heard of… Then we go into a clip of Kanye West." There's an empty walkway: "this is literally an empty thing. It has nothing to do with anything."

- **Cause:** five videos (the Monkey stream, the Steiny podcast, Evolution of N3ON, the premiere stream, the chatroom video) cover almost all of the first six minutes. The Monkey stream alone has 6 shots in the first minute. Sources were found from the script's own excerpts and title matches, not from the events the lines name. The premiere range included its establishing frames.
- **Teach:** `research.md`.
  - Show the moment itself: search for the event the line names.
  - Fame lines show the most recognisable people first.
  - Look at the exact seconds you place.
  - Use several videos per chapter.
- **Audit:** `commentary_source_overuse` flags three shots from one video (excerpts of one YouTube id count as one) inside 60 s. It reported 14 such stretches on R3.

### 2. Two graphics on repeat
"I was under the impression that we had thousands of After Effects motion graphics style references. Yet we use background blur, a box in the middle… The second one is just text in the middle, and that's it." People's photos as framed cards: "I'm not sure about us using this sort of picture style for showing images of these people."

- **Cause:** the agent had card, text, chapter, profile and look. The reference's other moves (349 AE comps and the Evil Design Premiere XML) had no operations: the YouTube reference, the dark-post highlight, punch-ins, the corner label, the real light leak and the channel bumper.
- **Code, new plan ops in `commentary_graphics.ts`:**
  - `commentary_youtube`, fed by the new `youtube_reference` tool (yt-dlp metadata, thumbnail and channel avatar; Repack's Data API needs a key Edit doesn't have).
  - `commentary_label`.
  - `commentary_bumper`, using the brand avatar and Subscribe button cropped from the channel page.
  - `commentary_punch`.
  - `commentary_leak`, the bundled real light-leak asset at the forensics recipe: rotated, Screen, 350%, 10 frames before and 12 after the cut.
  - `highlight_evidence dark:true` (Screen) for X posts.
  - `search_broll {queries:[]}` now returns the whole brand kit.
- **Teach:** `cut-ins.md`, `intro.md`, `transitions.md` and `notes.md`.
  - Choose from the whole vocabulary.
  - Cards are for evidence, not for people.
  - Punch-ins on the words that earn them.
  - The comic 2x plus label.
  - The bumper, "Let's get right to it", leaks with their sound.
- **Audit:** `commentary_graphics_monotony` fires when 85% or more of 10+ graphics are cards or centred text. On R3 it was 75 of 75, with 0 punch-ins.

### 3. Mistakes in the cut
- "A quick 1-second frame of neon, and then it goes over to Megan Fox… We cannot have mistakes like that." Two cards left a 2-frame gap, and the card's fade showed the footage under them.
- The clip around 1:15 starts "a second or two" before its natural start. The 2K clip at 2:36 cuts "abruptly in a very weird, unnatural way."
- **Cause:** Writer's commentary excerpts came over without cue words, so `realign_script` never snapped them to their quotes. They kept the script's whole-second times. Cards were as short as 1.4 s, mostly slide-in.
- **Code:** Writer quotes become the excerpt's cue words. `realign` also reads an older project's "Expected words" note.
- **Audits:** `commentary_flash` (a picture up for under 8 frames between two others; 7 on R3) and `commentary_short_card` (under 2.5 s; 7 on R3). Both join the automatic follow-up.

### 4. Silence in the clips
"The majority of the silence is cut out from the video… word, word, word, word… unless the silence makes sense for dramatic effect."

- **Code:** `tighten_voiceover footage:true` cuts the script excerpts' own gaps (picture and sound together, jump cuts). Defaults: pauses over 0.4 s cut down to 0.15 s.
- **Teach:** `edit-workflow.md` step 3 runs `realign_script`, then the footage tightening.

### 5. The weird blur
The card at 0:50 had blur bands across it.

- **Cause:** `blur_marks` blurred the burned-in title of the card's blurred backdrop. That blur lies above every picture, so it cut across the sharp card. The card's own marks could also reach past its edge.
- **Code:** a card's backdrop gets no mark blur, and an inset picture's blur is clipped to the picture. The user: "we would blur it within the square". A thumbnail with a big title is also no longer a person picture (pattern 2).
