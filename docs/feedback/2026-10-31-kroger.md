# Kroger import, Oct 31

"Kroger Employee Follows 5-Year-Old To The Bathroom, Thinks No One Is Watching" (script b6855669, a compilation of three YouTube videos) stopped while downloading at 5%, on three Macs (M's, Mathias's, kenobi): "yt-dlp stopped part-way (exit 1)". Sphe: "we're just so tired now".

## Fixed on the way

1. **Downloading: the YouTube stream couldn't survive a drop.** Cause: since Oct 30, script imports streamed YouTube through `--downloader ffmpeg` to stdout. That's one connection at about real time (~1.3×, so ~20 min for this 25-minute video) that nothing could resume, so one drop stopped the whole import. Fix: yt-dlp's own downloader into scratch, then upload, then delete (`writer/streamed.ts`). A drop past yt-dlp's retries goes to the next route and resumes (`youtube.ts` `partWay`). Commit 8c25e077. Verified: the Kroger video came down in 28 s (513 MB); a new test covers the drop and the next route; the other two videos imported in about a minute each.
2. **Downloading: a stored original "couldn't be read as video".** Cause: the old stream's upload this morning had left a complete but fragmented MP4 in the cloud, registered as ready. FFmpeg needs more than the check's minute to read its length over the network, so the import dropped it. Fix: a YouTube original that doesn't read is downloaded again over it, and a failed upload unmarks it as ready. Commit 597f55ae. Verified: the Retry downloaded it again and uploaded it (513 MB), and the import went on.
3. **Any step: a restart left the import stopped.** Cause: imports cut off by a restart or update were marked "stopped when Edit closed" and waited for Retry. Fix: they carry on by themselves 15 s after Edit starts again. Commit 01bd71b9. Verified: test, then a real dev-server reload mid-import; it carried on 15 s later.
