## What changed, by kind (30 commits, 2be068c6 to the write-up; all local, nothing pushed)

### Scripts
- **Teach:** house rules now go to interrogation scripts too; the first clip is the title's moment with picture, a line that lands, never small talk, a greeting, an order or a sound-only clip (replaces the guide's calm opening, said through `plain()` so Repack's guide file stays word for word); blocks about 3 min at most (4 in compilations), no cut-in after under a minute; cut-ins turn a scene rather than describe it again, carry what the footage can't, introduce people first; no crumbs; "child" kept out of narration; a "When" compilation's opening lines each take one case in story order (9fda48c9, fba7f6c5, 6921f70d, 63dc9f77).
- **Audit:** `paceFlags` in the build's own check (sound-only first clip, long and short blocks, crumbs, "child"), so a plan that misses them goes back once (9fda48c9).
- **Code:** the editor's mute notes on every footage clip with sex or rape-type words, after builds and every revision (9fda48c9, 101ed3fa, 4a9dcfc0, c7ff7b76); a Google Doc linked in a build's note is read and given to the builder (05b118d0: Chairman's "I couldn't open your reference doc").
- **New: Fresh eyes for scripts.** After a build, a second Opus turn reads the saved script as the team would and fixes what it'd flag, written straight in (~2 min). Built as an experiment (0eadaa0e), measured with and without on four scripts, on by default since (269e413c); asks a courtroom what its own shape asks (1cb5a88c, after it pushed 3-min blocks onto Trooper); its report says what it changed, not "proposed" (3e8ce790).

### Edits
- **Code defaults:** date_title puts the date first (2be068c6); a pause holds the zoom the shot has on that frame, and continues the shot it cuts from (3592d6f9, b4eb24cf); blur_marks never patches a photo inside our windows (0d7b47b6); a File Viewer stays whole on screen (27606c5a); a timed-out map render goes again once by itself (39a81f52); image search keeps the other queries' pictures when one page times out (b9643a2c); caption_speakers falls back to Claude when Codex is out of credit (7c4713f2).
