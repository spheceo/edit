## Where an edit's time goes (this Mac's chats)

Every long turn here (`~/.edit/projects/*.chat.json`, loop 7's rounds), by tool time and model time:

- A single-case "start the editing workflow" on the current builds (loop 7, Nov 1–2): **19–48 min, 63–97 tools, about half in tools and half in the model**, plus 2–7 min of fresh eyes.
- A comp is several of those in one: the "Off-Duty Officer" comp (Oct 30) took **2 h 29 min of agent time over 15 turns**, the longest 42 min; three of the turns were carried on after a restart and three were the audit's automatic follow-ups.
- The steadiest tool cost: **add_subtitles, 4–9 min of every edit** (2–3 calls of 2–4 min). Its log: one Opus call per footage clip, one after another, each 5–15 s for a 1 KB question ("putting spilled words back", turns.ts).
- Older sinks already fixed on main: map_view (43 min in the Oct 30 comp; now it waits at most 60 s for a first frame), get_edit_context (22 s a call on that comp then; 1.4 s on main).
- Still large: every get_edit_context on a comp answers ~75k characters (~19k tokens: footage blocks 20k, the 214 media 18k, cut-ins 13k, audit 13k), and the agent reads it dozens of times a turn. That's what fills the context to its compaction point and makes each model call slow.

## Fixed

- **add_subtitles' turn fixes are asked side by side** (turns.ts `AT_ONCE` = 6 across callers, the same question asked once; captions.ts `spokenLines` and subtitles.ts `repackSubtitles` run every clip's at once, lines in the same order). Measured with real Opus calls: 12 one-minute stretches of a real interrogation transcript, **97.6 s one after another, 19.0 s side by side**, every answer in (one borderline line answered differently, as the model does run to run). The whole Off-Duty comp (2 h 2 min, 1,345 lines): 87 calls, **add_subtitles in 2.0 min** (one after another, ~12 min).
- **Agent time on this edit**: a line over the agent panel, every turn added up (the workflow, its follow-ups, a turn carried on after a restart), ticking while one runs, never the waits between turns (edit_time.ts, AgentPanel's `EditTime`). The live turn already says "Working for 4m 12s" (0645befc, after nightly-49: JJ hasn't had it yet).
- **Each Mac's report lists its edits' agent time** ("Agent time", the last week's: total, turns, the longest, tool time and the tools that took longest), so the team's edit times can be compared from here without asking.

## Open (Sphe's call)

1. **A nightly**: JJ is on nightly-47; the timer, the edit total and the subtitle speed-up reach the team only with one.
2. **A smaller get_edit_context for comps** (the model's half): the media list and footage blocks only for the section asked, or once per turn. It changes what the agent reads, so it needs a trial against the loop's answer key before it ships.
3. **Fewer automatic follow-up turns**: each audit follow-up is a new 10–30 min turn; folding the audit into the workflow's final pass would save one or two of them on most edits, again only with a trial.
4. **Restarts mid-turn**: three of the Oct 30 comp's turns were carried on after a restart (each re-reads its context); updates already wait for an idle Mac, so these were crashes or manual restarts worth looking for in the team's reports.
