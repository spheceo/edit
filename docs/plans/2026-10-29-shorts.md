# Shorts (Oct 29)

The team: "give the script writing tool a source video and then we say that we want to find the highlights and cut it down to around 45 to 60 seconds. After that we can give it to the editing tool and make it a 9:16 format. We essentially just want to use the same subtitles as we do right now for the body cam. The music is a bit different." Style: youtube.com/@InOutNetwork/shorts, "with our own subtitles". "What the agent has to understand: making keyframes so we move around on the screen and always center what has to be centered."

## The reference, measured (six of InOutNetwork's most watched)

- 30–47 s; a hard cut every 2–8 s (scene detection: 4–24 cuts); no transitions, title card or end screen. The first frame is the story; the last is the payoff.
- 1080×1920: the 16:9 bodycam cropped to its height (~1.8× upscale), the crop following the subject inside a shot.
- Subtitles a word or three, white bold with a soft dark glow, the speaker's coloured label above (Officer green/yellow, Victim cyan, Suspect red), a little below the middle; they cut straight from one to the next (60 fps frames: no pop or fade). Swears starred. Action lines in the subtitle style when something happens without words ("Running Away", "Chasing", "Falls Down").
- The person introduced once: a freeze frame cut in and brightened, "WANTED / SUSPECT" in heavy white capitals with a deep shadow (the second line bigger, italic), a white hand-drawn arrow to them, ~1 s.
- Bodycam stamps and the uploader's marks blurred.

## In Edit

- `shorts` style (edit-core `Style::Shorts`): 1080×1920 by default, "SHORTS"/"SHORT" script headings, `edit.shorts` capability (relay manifest: needs a relay deploy for non-admins), no narration (`NARRATION.shorts = "none"`), no research turn after the import.
- `reframe` (reframe.ts): fills the frame and keyframes x from the face cache: the biggest face, then the same person; holds inside the middle, eased moves when they'd leave it; `centres` where no face is seen.
- `shorts_callout` (shorts.ts): the freeze-frame callout. Upright (Edit's text has no italic yet).
- `import_music` (shorts.ts): a song from a YouTube link laid under the whole Short, 20 LU under the speech, fading out. The team's songs: Minari "Jacob and the Stone", Mother Mother "Verbatim", knoa! "GO STYLE" (in the style's music-and-sound skill).
- Subtitles in a 9:16 project: lines of at most three words and 16 letters, a little below the middle (`subtitleRow`).
- The audit's Shorts checks: short_length, short_unfilled, short_subject_off, short_unsubtitled, short_no_music, short_music_stops.
- agent/styles/shorts: the notes and the workflow (highlights → remove_time → reframe → blur → subtitles and callout → music → final pass).

First run (She Drank 21 Bottles of Vanilla Extract, 19 min source): 47.6 s cut in one turn, reframed, callout, Verbatim under it.
