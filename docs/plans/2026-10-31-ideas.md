## Shape

- Home has an **Ideas** tab for people with `ideas.interrogation` (Edit's
  manifest). It lists what needs you (changes requested on the board, a run that
  stopped, a case already on the board), drafts, ready ideas and sent ones (with
  the list their card is on now). **New idea**: one or more YouTube or article
  links, an optional angle and summary; one idea per link, repackaged at once,
  three at a time.
- **The idea page** (`/ideas/<id>`): the titles (the starred one names the
  card), the thumbnail guide and its images, the case links, the footage folder,
  the summary; the chat with Claude beside it; Send to Trello / Update the card.
- **No research page yet**: no crawling. Video details come from the YouTube
  Data API on the relay (its key never leaves it); captions are fetched only
  through the YouTube proxy, never from the Mac's own address; an article is
  read as Script mode reads one.
