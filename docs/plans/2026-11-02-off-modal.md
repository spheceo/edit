# Edit off Modal: everything on the team's Macs, still cloud-shared

Sphe, Nov 2: Edit moves off Modal. Imports, playback and exports run on the team's Apple Silicon Macs (Sphe's M1, 8 GB, 15 GB free; JJ's M4 Pro, 24 GB), and everything stays shared through the cloud. The audit and its measurements are in the T3 thread "Apple Silicon Edit Speed vs Modal". Each step ships and is committed on its own, measured before and after.

## 1. Cache budget sized to the disk

Before: one budget of 400 MB for everything Edit keeps (storage.ts), made for a Mac that left its heavy work to Modal. This M1 on Nov 2: 15 GB free, a 6.7 GB cache (3.2 GB of it an export's chunks, 0.59 GB of cloud copies). Every hour the cleaner was 6.3 GB over, so every cloud copy older than six hours went (0.62 GB here), whoever's it was, and playing a project again read its previews from R2 again: the log's hourly "freed" lines run 0.01–0.67 GB on Oct 31 and Nov 1.

After: `localBudgetBytes` is a quarter of the room (free space plus the cache), between 400 MB and 50 GB, and always leaves 1 GB free: 5.4 GB on this M1. `EDIT_CACHE_BUDGET_MB` still overrides. Past it, copies of R2 objects go by project: footage no project here uses, then a done (Home's Done) or archived project's, then the projects this Mac opened longest ago (`opened.ts`, written when the editor opens a project and every minute it stays open). A project open now or in the last twelve hours keeps all of its; with the disk under 1 GB free, only one open in the last ten minutes does. Block folders now record their key, so a playback render's blocks are known to be its project's.

## 3. Export slots by encode engines

Before: one export per 12 GB of memory (export_capacity.ts), so JJ's 24 GB M4 Pro ran two at once, and they shared its one video encode engine: that's the encoder stall of Nov 2. Measured since: an Apple chip has one encode engine (M1–M4 and the Pros), two on a Max, four on an Ultra. After: one export per engine from the chip's name (`machdep.cpu.brand_string`: "Apple M1" → 1 here; EDIT_LOCAL_EXPORTS still overrides), still gated by memory and load. A second export waits and says why: "Queued · waiting for the encoder (this Mac has one)" (`waiting` on the export's activity, shown in the Exports panel).
