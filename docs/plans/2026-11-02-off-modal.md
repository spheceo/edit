# Edit off Modal: everything on the team's Macs, still cloud-shared

Sphe, Nov 2: Edit moves off Modal. Imports, playback and exports run on the team's Apple Silicon Macs (Sphe's M1, 8 GB, 15 GB free; JJ's M4 Pro, 24 GB), and everything stays shared through the cloud. The audit and its measurements are in the T3 thread "Apple Silicon Edit Speed vs Modal". Each step ships and is committed on its own, measured before and after.

## 1. Cache budget sized to the disk

Before: one budget of 400 MB for everything Edit keeps (storage.ts), made for a Mac that left its heavy work to Modal. This M1 on Nov 2: 15 GB free, a 6.7 GB cache (3.2 GB of it an export's chunks, 0.59 GB of cloud copies). Every hour the cleaner was 6.3 GB over, so every cloud copy older than six hours went (0.62 GB here), whoever's it was, and playing a project again read its previews from R2 again: the log's hourly "freed" lines run 0.01–0.67 GB on Oct 31 and Nov 1.

After: `localBudgetBytes` is a quarter of the room (free space plus the cache), between 400 MB and 50 GB, and always leaves 1 GB free: 5.4 GB on this M1. `EDIT_CACHE_BUDGET_MB` still overrides. Past it, copies of R2 objects go by project: footage no project here uses, then a done (Home's Done) or archived project's, then the projects this Mac opened longest ago (`opened.ts`, written when the editor opens a project and every minute it stays open). A project open now or in the last twelve hours keeps all of its; with the disk under 1 GB free, only one open in the last ten minutes does. Block folders now record their key, so a playback render's blocks are known to be its project's.

## 3. Export slots by encode engines

Before: one export per 12 GB of memory (export_capacity.ts), so JJ's 24 GB M4 Pro ran two at once, and they shared its one video encode engine: that's the encoder stall of Nov 2. Measured since: an Apple chip has one encode engine (M1–M4 and the Pros), two on a Max, four on an Ultra. After: one export per engine from the chip's name (`machdep.cpu.brand_string`: "Apple M1" → 1 here; EDIT_LOCAL_EXPORTS still overrides), still gated by memory and load. A second export waits and says why: "Queued · waiting for the encoder (this Mac has one)" (`waiting` on the export's activity, shown in the Exports panel).

## 2. Exports read local excerpts

Before: an export of a cloud edit read every original through a presigned URL, one FFmpeg process per clip, each restart paying R2's ~0.25–0.5 s from South Africa. Kroger (148,463 frames, 414 clips, 63 originals used, 2.1 GB of them) took 65 min 11 s on this M1 on Nov 1 (38 fps).

After: before rendering, every 30 s cell of an original the timeline shows is cut to the cache, picture stream-copied, starting on the original's frame grid (`export_excerpts.ts`); the engine's reader of that URL reads the local excerpt where one holds the time (`crates/edit-engine/src/excerpts.rs`, `VideoReader::read_excerpt`), with AVFoundation. FFmpeg's stream copy writes an edit list so the excerpt's time 0 is the cell's start exactly; AVFoundation and FFmpeg both honour it (render.rs tests: frame for frame with B-frames and a cut off a keyframe, and with a GOP longer than a cell at 29.97 fps). Chunk keys don't change (the scene and the URL's object are the same), so re-exports still copy their unchanged chunks. Kroger's 239 cells of 91 originals (1.6 GB) were cut in 45–69 s cold, found in 8.5 s warm.

Measured: the render of Kroger itself is not faster. Clean (no swapping, 23:02–23:16) it ran at ~32 fps; Modal's run of it was ~21 fps a GPU section, so Kroger is a heavy timeline, and its time goes to compositing and to the native reader starting a new AVAssetReader on every jump (`mac_media.m restart`), not mostly to the network. On the fixed build (Nov 3, 00:29–01:46, under load 16–44 from other threads' builds and servers, so not a clean number) Kroger exported whole in 77 min 00 s (32.1 fps) through one memory failure on this 8 GB Mac in swap and one false stall restart (fixed, 95837147); its last attempt composited 104,992 frames in 42 min 03 s and waited 1 min 40 s on decoding and 32 min 03 s on encoding: with the excerpts, decoding no longer limits it; the encoder's side does (readback, swizzle, VideoToolbox: step 7). Every one of its 368 cuts shows the same frames as Nov 1's export over URLs (a first pass flagged 5; each checked frame by frame, -2 to +2, identical). Chunk keys don't change with excerpts: two runs on one build reused each other's 21 chunks; a new build changes every key by design (the code's hash is in it).

Found and fixed on the way (each on Kroger, each tested): the addon written in place under a running export by `just dev`'s watch (8c3faf1f); the compositor's error hidden behind "the export stopped part-way through a chunk" (8c3faf1f); stills sent to the engine as URLs, which ImageIO can't read ("ImageIO couldn't decode the image", 17 min in: they're fetched to the cache first); a frame asked a hair past an original's last cell read from a URL reader with nothing decoded ("could not decode <url>: failed to fill whole buffer": the last cell reaches on past the end); the stall watch taking an engine retry's lower progress for no progress (95837147).

Open: excerpts in R2, shared between Macs, once uplink isn't the bottleneck (Kroger's took longer to upload than to cut); an A/B of the same spans over URLs and from excerpts on a quiet Mac; the reader's AVAssetReader per jump (a reader that seeks instead).

## 4. Smooth playback on the Mac

Before: a cloud edit's smooth playback rendered on Modal; with Modal off, here, as one file the viewer could play only once it was whole. Sphe watched "Smooth playback 59%" on the 26-minute 16-Year-Old (48,112 frames): the render took 16 min 49 s here (48 fps), and the first 2 minutes of it were the sound's mix before any picture.

After: (a) a render of the same picture already in R2 (Modal's) is played at once; (c) one Modal can't make is made here; (d) the render after an agent's turn runs here, behind exports; (e) kept at the project's size and "small" (the native chunked writer takes no other size; a scaled output comes with step 7); (f) the render does the chunks from the viewer's playhead first (`Plan.focus`: the minute from it, then the sound's mix, then the rest), writes its plan as it starts, and the viewer plays each chunk as it lands (`playback_stream.ts`, `lib/smoothStream.ts`: a MediaSource of fragmented-MP4 chunks around the playhead, the sound in an `<audio>` kept in step). Modal is off by default (`EDIT_MODAL_EXPORTS`, the Justfile and the trial server too). (b), a Mac's own render in R2, is held: whole it's ~900 MB up from South Africa after every turn; chunk by chunk later.

Measured on the 16-Year-Old: a finished render shows at a seek in 0.5–1.6 s and plays at 30 fps (146–151 frames in 5 s); a render under way showed its first chunk 17 s after it started (142 s before, the whole mix first); on a Mac with nothing cached, under load (another thread's builds and server), 138 s, the proxies' blocks read from R2 for the first time. Found on the way: a chunk out of timeline order took the next frame into its encoder ("Native encoder: The operation could not be completed"), a half-written mix served as the sound, a mix from another process taken for this render's, and a page that never asked again after a server restart; all fixed and tested.

## 9. Export retention

Rule (Sphe, Nov 2): the newest export of each project stays (re-exports copy from it); an older one goes once a newer export of the project is a week old, shared or not, its entry kept in exports.json and marked removed with where it still is (the review link's version, or Drive); with the disk under the 1 GB floor, older ones go at once, the oldest first, until there's room. Never the newest, never one exporting, being read, sharing or uploading, only files in Edit's export folders and on the list. Runs with the cache's cleaner; `bun scripts/export-retention.ts [home] [--delete]` reports (or deletes) from a data folder's files.

Measured on this Mac: the report on ~/.edit (32 projects) found nothing to delete, and the deleting run deleted nothing and left exports.json as it was: every export this Mac made has been archived to the cloud (~/Movies/Edit holds 7.8 MB), so Kroger's v4–v6 are on another Mac. The dev and trial homes: nothing either.

## The two bugs left from the first run

**A restart while an export is archiving re-ran the whole export.** The export's job (`jobs.json`) ended only after its file was uploaded to R2, so a restart during the upload started the export over (Kroger: 77 minutes, from nothing). Now the export's job ends when its file is whole and listed, and the upload is a job of its own (`archive`), which a restart sends again (`archiveFinished`), never rendering. Tested: while the upload runs, the list holds the archive and not the export; resuming it uploads and lists, nothing renders.
